Using Sprachpfad

Terms of use

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Sprachpfad is a free, independent German practice website. Use it responsibly, keep your account secure and treat its results as practice feedback.

The service

These terms cover the Sprachpfad website at sprachpfad-learn-deutsch.pages.dev, operated by Rohan Kumar Singh. Questions can be sent to rohank.singh2002@gmail.com.

The site provides German A1–B1 explanations, exercises, vocabulary review, listening material, study planning and practice tests. Its current features are free to use and do not require payment details.

Accounts and saved progress

You may practise as a guest or choose Google sign-in. Use only an account you are authorised to access and sign out on shared devices. Keep a downloaded copy of progress that matters to you: browser storage can be cleared, connections can fail and service interruptions can occur.

If two devices have competing saves, the website asks which copy to keep. Review that choice carefully. Resetting progress affects the active save; it does not delete your sign-in account. To request account deletion, contact the operator.

The Privacy policy explains how personal information and learning records are handled.

Responsible use

Do not attempt to access another person's account or progress, bypass access controls, disrupt the service, or use it for unlawful activity. Access may be restricted where reasonably necessary to protect the service or its users. Contact the operator if you believe a restriction is a mistake.

They do not certify language proficiency or guarantee an examination result. Materials may contain mistakes; please report problems so they can be reviewed.
