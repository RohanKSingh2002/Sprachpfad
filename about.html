Sprachpfad is a free website for building your German, one lesson at a time. Practise grammar, vocabulary, listening and everyday communication at your own pace.

03 / RETURN

Keep your progress

Practise on one device as a guest, or use Google sign-in to save your learning progress across devices.

Your choice of account

Google sign-in is optional. Choose Continue on this device to practise without an account. If you sign in, Sprachpfad uses your Google identity to recognise you and saves your learning progress privately through Supabase. See our Privacy policy for the data involved and how to request deletion.
