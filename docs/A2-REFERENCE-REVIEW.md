## Teaching decisions

- Keep past ability (konnte) distinct from polite or hypothetical possibility (könnte).
- Explain that als can refer to a single past period as well as a brief event; repeated occasions use wenn.
- Teach ob as whether, separately from conditional wenn.
- Choose adjective endings from case, gender, number and article pattern. Introduce frequent article-free phrases before expecting the full declension system.
- Teach verbs with their prepositions and cases as complete phrases. Distinguish questions about people from questions about things or topics without a blanket living/non-living rule.
- Teach reflexive case through contrasting patterns. Another noun in a sentence does not automatically make the reflexive pronoun dative.
- Give writing and speaking tasks communicative goals, models and revision steps. They require feedback and are not automatically scored for language proficiency.
- Revisit useful structures in B1 with longer tasks. The notebook's topic list is not used as a checklist certifying complete A2 coverage.
