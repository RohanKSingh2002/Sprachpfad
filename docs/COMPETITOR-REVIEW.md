## Changes in Sprachpfad

- **Course continuity.** The Lessons page leads with the next unfinished lesson or checkpoint and its course progress. The course description moved into a disclosure, so the action and week cards are easier to find.
- **Practice navigation.** The existing Practice page opens with direct links to flashcards and sentence practice. It shows the count of saved mistakes and routes lesson mistakes into focused retry rounds. The introductory banner and its extra collection link were removed.
- **Review based on answers.** A correct answer clears the corresponding lesson mistake from device storage. Grammar topics with missed answers sort first, followed by unfinished topics. Lesson first-try scores still follow the original scoring rules.
- **Listening and display.** Sentence translation and dual audio sit beside playback. One disclosure contains the advanced audio controls. Lesson listening questions have a slower replay that uses 75% of the current speed, with a 0.5× floor. Study Plan saves a choice between the lesson list and one exercise at a time.

Chapter checkpoints, sentence collections, the five-level ratings and the global speed preference already covered several patterns from the comparison.

## Signed-in Duolingo follow-up

- **Course structure.** The German course overview showed eight numbered sections and a Daily Refresh that unlocks at course completion. It explicitly marked Sections 1–3 as A1, Section 4 as A2 and Section 5 as B1. Section details describe what learners can say at that stage and list grammar topics. The path offers jump tests for later units and sections. Lesson nodes show the lesson title and its position before opening. The first unit advanced to lesson 2 of 4 after the test run.
- **Guidebook.** The first unit header opens a compact reference with playable German phrases, English translations and a grammar tip with a small reference table. The guidebook is reachable directly from the path. [German course path](https://www.duolingo.com/learn)
- **Exercise sequence.** The first lesson presented 11 original questions and one retry. It combined numbered vocabulary choices, meaning recognition and ordered translation tiles. A word introduced on its own returned in progressively longer phrases. Translation prompts included audio playback.
- **Feedback and layout.** A progress bar stays above the exercise, while the bottom bar holds Check and then Continue. Checking locks the submitted response and focuses Continue. Feedback can show a correct solution and offers difficulty feedback and reporting. A deliberately missed question changed the on-screen allowance from five to four, then returned in a labelled review step before completion. A five-answer milestone appeared between questions.
- **Completion and review.** The results screen showed XP and accuracy. Its optional scorecard presented each attempt as a tile and revealed the submitted and correct responses on click. The next screens introduced the German Score, showed a streak update, and offered a streak goal. The audit completed one lesson and stopped before selecting a goal.
- **Practice and progress.** Radio, path Practice and Unit Review displayed unlock requirements. The visible web navigation had no standalone Practice tab. The Quests page did link to a separate practice session whose first question paired German audio clips with English meanings and offered a control to skip listening. Quests showed a daily XP goal and refresh timer. Profile showed streak, total XP and achievements. [Quests](https://www.duolingo.com/quests), [Duolingo’s Practice tab guide](https://blog.duolingo.com/guide-to-duolingo-practice-hub/)
- **Settings.** Preferences grouped sound effects, animations, motivational messages and listening exercises under Lesson experience, with a separate system, light or dark appearance choice. Notifications offered weekly progress and a scheduled daily email reminder. Courses displayed the enrolled language and a disabled reset button. Privacy exposed profile visibility, personalised ads and Friend Streaks. The review left the settings as found. [Web preferences](https://www.duolingo.com/settings/account)

### Improvements to consider for Sprachpfad

1. **Exercise scorecard.** Add an optional scorecard beside the existing retry action in the lesson summary. Let learners open an item to compare their first answer with the correction. Preserve the first-try score after a successful retry.
2. **Consistent Check and Continue.** In one-at-a-time mode, let learners review their selection before submitting it. Keep progress, feedback and the next action visible on short screens. This would make choice, typing and word-building exercises behave more consistently.
3. **Week guidebook.** Add a reference inside each week card on the Lessons page. Use Sprachpfad’s existing learning goals, vocabulary, dialogue audio and grammar notes. Start with a practical outcome the learner can check for themselves.

4. **Audio matching and listening alternatives.** Add audio-to-meaning pairs under Exercise. For ordinary listening rounds, offer a per-session way to switch to reading practice when audio is inconvenient. Keep exam listening assessments audio-based.

5. **Lesson experience controls.** Put controls for sound effects, celebrations and motion alongside the existing audio speed setting where applicable. Let learners keep spoken German while turning off feedback sounds. Use the existing reminder and profile screens for study goals and progress.

Direct exercise observations come from the first German lesson, the start of lesson two and the practice preview. Later units, Radio playback, the Unit Review exercises, native app screens and subscription features remain outside this hands-on review.
